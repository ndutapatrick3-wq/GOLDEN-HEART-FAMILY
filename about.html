WHO WE ARE

Golden Heart Family

Golden Heart Family is a charity group dedicated to bringing hope, love and support to children's homes. We believe every child deserves to feel loved, safe and given a chance for a brighter future.

We started in 2024 as a WhatsApp group of people who wanted to make a difference. So far we have visited 4 children's homes, and our 5th visit is coming up.

Together we can do more ♡

What we do

We visit children's homes and bring food, clothing and school supplies. We spend time with the children, play games, celebrate with cake, and spread love and compassion where it's needed most.

We have no physical office. We work across communities through visits and events.
